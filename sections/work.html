RiskMulate

A browser-based 3D simulation that teaches risk management inside an interactive industrial facility.

I am building RiskMulate around the full risk cycle, with first-person exploration, risk identification, a live register, a 5×5 likelihood-impact matrix, treatment decisions and feedback. The project combines game systems, product design and educational structure grounded in ISO 31000:2018 and COSO ERM.
