01 / HOW I THINK

An editorial eye
for startups.

I am at my best when something has promise but is not quite there yet.

I look at the product, the people using it, the resources available and the direction the team is taking. Then I ask a simple set of questions: What is working? What is missing? What is creating friction? What deserves another iteration? What should stop?

That judgment matters in early-stage teams where every week and every build decision carries a cost. I pair that product instinct with technical execution, community experience and a willingness to test ideas in public.

A

Spot the potential

I can see the useful core inside a rough idea and separate it from the parts that distract from the real value.

B

Find the missing piece

I notice gaps in product flow, positioning, user experience, community structure and execution that can keep a good idea from landing.

C

Call the wrong fit early

I am comfortable saying when a feature, idea or direction is consuming effort without earning its place.
